How your vehicle becomes credit

Your vehicle can be more than transportation.

Vehicle equity is the portion of your vehicle's value that you have already paid for. The provider can consider that equity alongside income, expenses, vehicle details and credit history.

01
Equity matters. The value and equity in your vehicle can support qualification.
02
Your credit history isn't the only factor. Multiple parts of your financial picture may be considered.
03
You keep driving. A lien may be recorded on the title while you keep possession of the vehicle.
What happens to your title

Your vehicle stays with you.

If approved, a lien can be recorded on the vehicle title as security for the account. You continue driving and maintaining the vehicle according to the provider's terms.

  • You keep possession of your vehicle.
  • You continue driving it.
  • Registration and insurance remain in your name.
  • The lien can be released after the account is settled under the applicable terms.

Questions people ask

Before they apply.

Is this a loan or a credit card?

It is positioned as a vehicle-backed revolving credit card / credit line rather than a simple one-time cash loan.

Does my credit have to be perfect?

Not necessarily. The provider considers multiple factors including vehicle information, income, expenses, ability to repay and credit history.

Can I keep driving my vehicle?

Yes. If approved, you generally keep possession and continue driving the vehicle while a lien may be recorded on the title.

Can I still qualify if I am paying off my vehicle?

Potentially. A refinance path may be available depending on your situation and the provider's requirements.

Why do I upload my credit screenshot here?

It is part of this landing page's custom customer journey. The external credit-check service is separate from the product provider. The screenshot is not presented as an official provider requirement.
