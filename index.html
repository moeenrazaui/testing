How your vehicle becomes credit

Your vehicle has value. That value can support your credit.

Vehicle equity is the portion of your vehicle's value that you have already paid for. A vehicle-backed credit product can consider that equity alongside income, expenses and credit history.

01

Good to know

What happens to your title — and what you need.

The goal is to make the product easy to understand before you decide whether to continue.

Your title

If approved, the provider records a lien on your vehicle title as security for the credit. You continue to own and drive the vehicle while the account is active.

  • Your vehicle remains with you.
  • You continue driving and maintaining it.
  • Registration and insurance remain in your name.
  • The lien can be released after the account is settled under the provider's terms.

What you need

Eligibility depends on the provider's review. Typical requirements include:

  • A safe, working vehicle
  • Vehicle title in your name
  • Valid government-issued identification
  • Income and ability to repay

Questions

A few things worth knowing.

Is this a loan or a credit card?

The product is positioned as a vehicle-backed revolving credit card / credit line. It is not simply a one-time cash loan.

Does my credit score have to be perfect?

Not necessarily. Multiple factors may be considered, including credit history, vehicle information, income, expenses and ability to repay.

Do I have to stop driving my vehicle?

No. If approved, you generally keep possession and continue driving the vehicle, while a lien may be recorded on the title under the account terms.

Can I qualify if I am still paying off my vehicle?

Potentially. Depending on the situation, a refinance option may be available. The provider makes the final determination.
